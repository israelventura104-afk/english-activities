My city, my favorite places

A short story before you start the activities

Reading · Listening · A1–A2
Listen to Diego · follow the text while you listen

My name is Diego. I am 16 years old, and I live in a busy city with my family. Our apartment is on Maple Street, next to a small park. In the morning the street is crowded, but at night it is usually quiet and safe.

What do I like most? The park and the cafe near the library. After school I walk to the park with my friends. Sometimes we sit near the fountain and talk. The library is across from the park, so when I need a book, I just cross the street.

On Saturdays I help my mom. We take the bus, wait at the bus stop, and go to the supermarket. There is a parking lot behind the supermarket and a pharmacy next to it. If we get lost, my mom always says, “Excuse me. How can I get to the supermarket?” People are friendly. They say, “Go straight, then turn left,” and we always find the way.

My city is not perfect. Some streets are dangerous at night, and the center is too crowded on weekends. But for me it is still beautiful and convenient. It is my city — and I am happy here.

Diego · 16 Maple Street Park · Library · Supermarket
Diego’s favorite place: the park next to his street

Think about it

  1. Where does Diego live, and what is his street like in the morning and at night?
  2. What places does he mention near the park and the supermarket?
  3. Talk about your city: What do you like most? Is it busy or quiet? What is next to your house?
