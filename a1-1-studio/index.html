02 · Follow the details

Reading

Read Sofia’s Saturday message, then connect the ideas.

Hi, I’m Sofia. I am a business student, and this is my family’s apartment. It is small, bright, and comfortable. There is a cozy living room next to the kitchen. There are two green plants near the window, and those books on the table are my brother’s.

Today my friend Maya is here. She is from Veracruz, and her favorite place in my neighborhood is the park. The park is across from the library and behind a small café. We want to go there this afternoon. From my apartment, we go straight on Maple Street and turn left at the pharmacy. The park is next to the bus stop. These are our water bottles, and that blue backpack by the door is Maya’s.

Before we leave, I look for my phone. It is under the books, next to my brother’s keys. Maya opens the window because the apartment is warm. We can hear children in the park and a bus stopping across the street. My brother is not coming with us; he is busy in the kitchen. He makes two sandwiches and puts them in Maya’s backpack. Now we have food and water for a quiet afternoon outside.
